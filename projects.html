Project N°2: Bitsy "Temperance"


I've been working on this one since one of my dearest ex-gf made me discover Bitsy games in 2022.
At first I just wanted to try the engine because it sounded fun to make a lil videogame without any knowledge about coding.
But quickly it turned into a story about the begining of my transition as trans feminine person, about some traumatic events medled with the depressing everyday life.

Project N°1: project leila-khaled


This project is a little website to help Dolls_Money .
Dolls_Money is an anarchist, feminist, anti-fascist, anti-colonialist and anti-Zionist initiative.
They made in 2024 the compilation "MIXTAPE PALESTINE LIBRE DE LA MER AU JOURDAIN" and are working on a new mixtape. All the money will go to support Palestine. No members are paid.

Project N°0: Exit the game


Rather than chasing an ideal, I'm learning to "slow down".
I'm trying to measure my worth differently: not by what I prove, but by what I feel, what I built and build, what I connect and relate to (that also means working on my anxiety issues).
My projects stem from the desire to experiment without having to justify, to create at my own pace (usually the longer it takes the more likely I give up), and to no longer seek validation.
