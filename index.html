What's all this?

I started writing my first website around 2023 with the goal to learn HTML and CSS. Then I learnt about Javascript against my will. Then I used it as a tool to promote myself. And in 2026 I made this new website based on the old one.

I am not very consistent regarding the efforts required to maintain this website — so don't expect much from it.

Why all this?

I keep this website as a personal, free, and lasting space. A place to share what I do and what inspires me: music, art, videos, books, and more. It's a place to showcase projects in progress or completed, keep a record of my explorations, and let my ideas evolve without external constraints.

This site isn't meant to be perfect or static. It exists to be alive, authentic, and a reflection of who I am.
